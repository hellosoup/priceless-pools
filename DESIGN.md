# Design directions

The audience owns an existing pool in South East Queensland or Northern NSW. The primary action is to share photos for a restoration enquiry. Services: restoration, painting on all surfaces, acid washing and pressure cleaning. Contact information and project photography remain placeholders.

## Architectural luxury
Palette: porcelain #f9faf8, deep water #12343d, ink #18383e, stone #dce2df, aqua #b5d7d9.
Type: Outfit light display, DM Sans body.
Layout: full-width photographic hero with left-aligned oversized typography and a quiet lower-right service note. Open service list, asymmetric gallery, dark contact section.
Wireframe: [navigation] [wide image + headline] [intro | services] [wide photo | two photos] [contact | form].
Principle: the stillness of clear water and the proportions of an architectural portfolio.

## Coastal boutique
Palette: mist #edf5f5, ocean #315a67, sky #ccdee3, white #ffffff, sand #e8e2d8.
Type: Lora regular display, DM Sans body.
Layout: left-aligned serif hero alongside a tall pool image with a pool-shaped curved corner. Soft blue sections, generous margins, staggered gallery.
Wireframe: [navigation] [headline | tall photograph] [three services] [staggered photographs] [contact | white form].
Principle: a relaxed Australian backyard, with texture and air instead of ornamental decoration.

## Bold specialist
Palette: navy #092c42, midnight #061e30, aqua #8be8e1, white #f3fafb, slate #a7becb.
Type: Barlow Condensed semibold display, Barlow body.
Layout: large condensed headline adjoining a strongly cropped pool photograph, compact service strip, edge-aligned gallery and high-contrast enquiry panel.
Wireframe: [navigation] [large statement | pool image] [service strip] [gallery grid] [aqua contact | dark form].
Principle: direct, capable and specific about restoring existing pools.

## Brief review
All directions share verified service copy but have distinct typography, hero composition, gallery proportions and contact treatment. Avoid invented testimonials, project credits, credentials and before/after transformations. Stock imagery is labelled as inspiration. The comparison control is preview chrome, separate from the customer website. Use one real primary action throughout: send pool photos.
